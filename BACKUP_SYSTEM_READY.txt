⚠️  CRITICAL ENCODING INFORMATION
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

UTF-8 WITH BOM IS MANDATORY FOR:
✓ Nepali Devanagari script (नेपाली)
✓ Japanese characters (日本語)
✓ Proper character display in browser

WITHOUT UTF-8 BOM:
✗ Nepali text becomes garbled (दोष्य रुप देखिन्छ)
✗ Some Japanese characters may be corrupted
✗ Browser display issues

FILE ENCODING CHECK:
• VS Code: Bottom right corner shows encoding
• Must be: "UTF-8 with BOM"
• When saving: "UTF-8 with BOM" encoding
